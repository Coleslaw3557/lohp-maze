# PhotoBomb sound pack

All deliverables are PCM WAV at 48 kHz and no longer than 15 seconds. The room combines an obnoxious-influencer centerpiece with camera-focused game and movie references.

## Recommended setup

- Play or rotate files from `background/` while the player enters and poses.
- Fire one file from `photo_triggers/` when the photo button or flash activates.
- `background/13_selfie_influencer_presence.wav` establishes the influencer joke and reaches “But first, let me take a selfie.” near its ending.
- `photo_triggers/18_selfie_then_mean_girls.wav` is a self-contained version of the complete centerpiece: monologue → selfie line → autofocus/shutter burst → “She doesn't even go here!”
- For a shorter live sequence, use raw clips `06`, `07`, and `08` directly after the background line.
